3-Day Buffer & Fast Load

3-Day Buffer notes are lightweight scratchpads saved locally in your browser. They automatically expire and clear after 72 hours unless you click +3 Days or Keep Forever.

Only the note you open loads its full body & images, keeping startup instant even with large notebooks.
