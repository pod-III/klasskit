How to Play Running Dictation

A high-energy 4-skills classroom favourite (Reading, Speaking, Listening & Writing)

Classroom Roles: Runner & Writer

Put students in pairs (or small teams). One student is the Writer (sits at the desk with the gapped Worksheet) and the other is the Runner. Tape the Wall Poster (or cut-out Sentence Strips) on the walls away from the desks. The Runner walks/runs to the wall, reads and memorizes a chunk, returns to the desk, and dictates it aloud (no shouting across the room, no photos!). Swap roles halfway through!

Preparing Your Handouts

  1. Source Text & Title: Paste a short story or numbered sentences in Step 1.
  2. Blanks: Click or drag across words in the Interactive Editor to make pink blanks, or use Random % / Every N-th Word (which automatically skips tiny stop words like the, and, is).
  3. Wall Format: Choose a Single Poster or Sentence Strips (numbered cut-out cards `#1, #2…` with dashed scissor lines to post around the room).
  4. Worksheet Clues: Scaffold for younger learners with 1st Letter Clues (d_______), Letter Count Dashes (_ _ _ _ _), or a Shuffled Word Bank.

Teacher Pro-Tips

  • Print 2–3 copies of the Wall Poster for larger classes so runners don't crowd one spot.
  • When using Sentence Strips, scatter them around the room so pairs hunt for `#1`, `#2`, `#3` in order.
  • When a pair finishes, check their spelling against the Teacher Key!
