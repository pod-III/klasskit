How to Play Mystery Tiles

Questions, Traps, and Gifts are randomly hidden behind numbered tiles. Teams take turns choosing a tile number—answer correctly to earn points, or brace yourself for a surprise!

Traps

  • Bomb! Lose 1× question points (min 0).
  • Snip snip! Lose half your score (rounded down).
  • Frozen! Skip your next turn.
  • Generous! Give up to 1× points to another team.

Gifts

  • Bonus! Instant 2× question points!
  • Double up! Double your total score!
  • Sneaky! Steal up to 1× points from a team.
  • Switcheroo! Swap scores with any team.
  • Go again! Keep your turn for another tile!
