How Paper Grass Works

  1. Type or paste target sentences (one per line), or pick a CEFR starter pack.
  2. Choose a puzzle style (Word Scramble, Caesar Cipher, Fix Error, No Vowels, Jumble, Word Glue, or Mixed).
  3. Print and cut along the dashed row lines, stopping at the green spine so the strips hang like blades of grass.
  4. Tape the uncut spine to a wall or board. Students grab a strip, rip it off, and solve the sentence puzzle!
