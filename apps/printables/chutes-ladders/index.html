How to Use Slides & Ladders

Printable A4 Boards & Live Smartboard Play

1. Custom Prompts & Action Tags

Write one prompt per line. Add | answer for Teacher Keys, or prefix lines with [+2], [-2], [ROLL], or [SKIP] to create interactive action squares. Click any tile on the board to inspect or edit it live!

2. Live Smartboard & Check Answer Mode

Play with 2–6 teams. Turn on Check Q to open each landed square with a 15-second timer and Correct / Miss judgment buttons: correct answers climb ladders or dodge slides!

3. A4 Printouts & Keyboard Shortcuts

Switch to B&W Ink-Saver in the Board tab, pick Portrait or Landscape, and print A4 Handouts (with optional cut-out counters) or Teacher Keys, or export PNG/SVG.
