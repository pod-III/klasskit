How to Use Chutes & Ladders

Printable A4 Boards & Live Smartboard Play

1. Customize Ladders & Chutes

Click New Links to auto-generate balanced paths, or click Edit Links and tap any two squares on the board: low → high draws a Ladder, high → low draws a Chute/Snake. Tap any existing link in Edit Mode to delete it.

2. Printable A4 Handouts & Keys

Add prompts one per line (use go | went for auto Answer Keys). Switch to B&W Print to save ink, pick Portrait or Landscape, and print a crisp A4 HANDOUT or KEY.

3. Live Smartboard Play

Collapse the left sidebar, pick 2–4 teams, and press ROLL DIE (or Spacebar). Counters hop square by square, automatically climb ladders or slide down chutes, and pop up the square's prompt card!
