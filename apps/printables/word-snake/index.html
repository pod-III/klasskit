How to Use Word Snake

Print Handouts & Interactive Smartboard Play

1. Printable A4 Worksheets

Enter your vocabulary words, choose a shape (Circle, Square, Rectangle, Serpentine, or Random Maze), and pick difficulty: Blend uses decoys purely from your word list, No Gaps chains words head-to-tail, and 1-PAGE, 2-PAGE, or KEY prints clean A4 handouts with pastel Answer Bands.

2. Live Smartboard & Student View

Open OPEN STUDENT VIEW on your projector, or let students tap letters directly on the snake! Click any pill in the right-hand Vocabulary panel to spotlight that word on the snake.
