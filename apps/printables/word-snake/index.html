How to Use Word Snake

Print Handouts & Interactive Smartboard Play

1. Printable A4 Worksheets

Enter your vocabulary words, choose a shape (Circle, Square, Rectangle, Serpentine, or Random Maze), and adjust the difficulty: use Sneaky Blend to generate decoy letters purely from your vocabulary words, or First-Letter Hints (`A _ _ _ _ (5)`) to make students recall spelling before finding the word!

2. Live Smartboard / Projector Play

Collapse the controls sidebar (`Controls` button) and invite students up to tap letters directly on the snake! When all letters of a hidden word are tapped, the word automatically checks itself off in the bank. Or click any word pill at the bottom to spotlight its exact location on the snake!
