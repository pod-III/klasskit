How Labyrinth Quest Works

Real Maze Escape + Point-Hunting Prompts

1. Find Your Way Out & Hunt Points

Students navigate a real walled labyrinth from START to EXIT. Inside the corridors and dead-end rooms are numbered point stars (1, 2, 3...). Reaching a star lets them complete that matching prompt to collect its points!

2. Risk & Reward Placement

In Risk & Reward mode, high-value treasure prompts (+40/+50 pts) are tucked into dead-end chambers, so students decide whether to rush straight to the exit or detour for bonus points.

3. Print Portrait or Landscape A4

Use HANDOUT for a clean A4 student sheet (maze + numbered prompt checklist + score box) and KEY to print the dashed escape route and answer key.
