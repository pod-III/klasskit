How to Use Maze Maker

Two Classroom Modes + Strict A4 Print

1. Word Path Maze (Grid Sorting)

Builds a single guaranteed non-touching path from START to FINISH filled with your Path Words, surrounded by Decoy Words. Ideal for irregular verbs, phonics sounds, or category sorting.

2. Wall Maze + Checkpoints

Generates a classic walled labyrinth and places your target words or letters along the true solution route while hiding decoys inside dead ends. Students trace the maze and record the checkpoints they collect.

3. Smartboard & A4 Print

Tap tiles or maze cells directly on screen to solve together as a class, tap any right-panel pill to spotlight it, or use HANDOUT and KEY to print clean A4 sheets.
